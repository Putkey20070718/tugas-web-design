Sop Ayam Recipe

Home Picture Table Recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 gram chicken
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 potatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 onion
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 1 liter water

How to Make Sop Ayam

  1. Boil the chicken in water.
  2. Add the carrots and potatoes.
  3. Add garlic and onion.
  4. Add salt and pepper.
  5. Cook until the vegetables are soft.
  6. Serve the Sop Ayam in a bowl.

Enjoy your Sop Ayam!
